# Interview Preparation Guide

## Typical hiring stages
Most tech hiring follows: resume screen, online assessment (aptitude/coding/SQL), one or two technical interviews, a managerial or behavioural round, and HR discussion (compensation, joining date). Data roles often add a take-home case study or dashboard task.

## Technical preparation
For software roles practise data structures and algorithms (arrays, strings, hashing, trees, graphs, dynamic programming) on platforms such as LeetCode or GeeksforGeeks, and be able to explain your own projects end to end. For data analyst roles practise SQL joins, GROUP BY, window functions and CTEs, Excel pivot tables and lookups, basic statistics (mean/median, distributions, hypothesis testing) and explaining a dashboard. For machine learning roles revise bias-variance, evaluation metrics, overfitting and regularisation, feature engineering, and one project you can defend in depth.

## Behavioural questions and the STAR method
Answer behavioural questions with STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Prepare 5-6 stories covering a challenge, a failure and what you learned, teamwork or conflict, leadership or initiative, and a deadline under pressure. Keep each answer to about two minutes and finish with a measurable result.

## Before and after the interview
Research the company's products and recent news, re-read the job description, and prepare two or three thoughtful questions for the interviewer. Test your setup for online interviews. Send a short thank-you note within 24 hours. Track every application, stage and outcome in a spreadsheet to spot patterns.
