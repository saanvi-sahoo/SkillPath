# First Job and Workplace Growth Guide

## The first weeks in a new job
Learn the team's goals, tools and who to ask for what. Take notes, ask questions early, and agree with your manager on what success looks like in the first 30, 60 and 90 days. Deliver one small, visible win early and share progress proactively rather than waiting to be asked.

## Communicating with your manager and team
Send short status updates (done, in progress, blockers), ask for feedback every few weeks, and clarify requirements before starting big tasks. When you are stuck for more than an hour or two, ask for help with what you already tried. Write clearly: one topic per message and the key point first.

## Asking for a raise or promotion
Prepare evidence: results with numbers, responsibilities you took on beyond your role, and market salary data for your role and city. Ask for a dedicated conversation, present your impact first, then your request, and if the answer is "not now", ask what specific goals would get you there and when to revisit.

## Growing your career while working
Keep learning one skill aligned with the next role you want, volunteer for projects that use it, find a mentor inside or outside the company, and update your resume every six months with new achievements so you are ready for opportunities.
